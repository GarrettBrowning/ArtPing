We protect your Art from AI

Your work belongs to you, not to AI companies. Here's what ArtPing does to keep it out of AI training:

  • We block AI training crawlers. Bots that collect images to train AI (like OpenAI's GPTBot, Anthropic's ClaudeBot, Common Crawl and ByteDance's Bytespider) are told to stay out, and turned away if they visit anyway.
  • We mark every image "do not train". Each piece is sent with "noai" signals that AI tools are asked to respect.
  • We strip hidden data. Location (GPS) and other hidden details are removed from every file you upload.
  • We show you how to protect your work itself. The Upload page has step-by-step guides to Glaze and Nightshade, free tools that make your work much harder for AI to learn from.

No website can fully stop a scraper that pretends to be a normal visitor, so we're open about what we can and can't do. We'll keep adding protections as better ones appear.
